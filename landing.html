San Diego County youth opportunity

Mapping youth opportunity deserts across San Diego County

The Youth Opportunity Index Dashboard is an interactive decision-support tool that maps where young people in San Diego County may face the greatest barriers to opportunity. It brings together education, housing, mobility, safety, economic, health, and youth-support indicators so users can see not only where need is concentrated, but also which barriers are driving the gap across tracts, ZIP codes, regions, and districts. By connecting opportunity scores with service locations, transit context, COI comparisons, and Workforce-report benchmarks, the dashboard helps communities and decision-makers identify where support and investment may be needed most.

Next phase

What still needs to be improved?

Refine service taxonomy

Review youth-support, wraparound, employment, and transition-pathway categories so the service layer better matches stakeholder expectations and the Workforce report.

Add exact regional validation metrics

Add Workforce-aligned regional values for unemployment, labor-force participation, graduation, dropout, postsecondary enrollment, credential attainment, and youth segment counts.

Strengthen documentation

Build a final data dictionary with source, year, geography, transformation, directionality, and domain assignment for every indicator.
